Speech text: —
Go straight. Turn left/right at the + place. Go past the + place.
Speaking Mission — M.2
Speak the route in 2–4 short, clear sentences. Use landmarks to make the listener reach the target, then name the destination.
Meaning and the real route are checked — not one memorized sentence.
Optional enrichment: You may also use next to, near, opposite, between when the relationship is true on the map. These words are not required for a correct answer.
Randomized test: Questions 1–10 are shuffled separately for each student, and the order stays fixed during the test.
Natural English accepted: go straight / straight ahead / keep going / continue • go past / pass • turn left/right • take/make a left/right • take the next/first/second left/right • at the bank, turn right • when you reach the bank, turn right. Optional location language such as next to / near / opposite / between is also accepted when correct. The system checks the meaning and the real route, not one memorized sentence.
Recommended speaking pattern
1 Go straight2 Turn / Go past...3 Continue if needed4 Name the destination
